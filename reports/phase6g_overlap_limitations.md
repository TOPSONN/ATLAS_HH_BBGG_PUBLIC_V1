# Phase6G background interpretation and overlap limits

The three proposed source streams are selected tree-level LO components: B04 contains qqbar-initiated ZH with the source Z decay to bb and a stable source Higgs; B05 contains ttH with stable source top, antitop and Higgs; B06 contains bb, one Born photon and one allowed j4 parton. Their event accounting, generator cross sections and weights must remain separate. No sum of these streams is a complete physical background prediction.

B04 omits loop-induced ggZH and NLO corrections. Its MG5 Zbb chain already includes the Z decay in the generated cross section. The Z branching ratio must not be applied again. B05 uses documented supported inclusive Pythia top/W decays for the bounded pilot; production-decay spin correlations are not validated. Rare supported CKM top decays to sW or dW remain part of that inclusive model and must not be silently replaced by forced bW decays. No exclusive top/W branching-ratio factor or single-Higgs-to-HH factor of two is introduced.

B04 and B05 use a forced Higgs gamma-gamma decay in Pythia. An actually initialized model Higgs branching ratio can enter a separately named `MODEL_BR_ONLY` contribution after real source/Info/converter closure proves which factor is missing. That factor acts on the original raw source contribution exactly once. Phase6F's Pythia model reference `0.0022965313147512443` and the task's YR4 comparison reference `0.002270` describe different conventions and remain separately labelled. A numerical model reference is not an officially adopted precision branching-ratio prediction with uncertainty.

B06 has one Born-level prompt photon. Secondary shower/radiation photons, hadron-decay photons and remaining photon origins are recorded separately through actual source and decay ancestry. A second reconstructed Delphes Photon object may arise in this model, but it does not establish a calibrated jet-to-photon misidentification rate. No artificial jet-to-photon replacement is used. The two-photon cutflow and the one-photon control cutflow can be reported as generated-model diagnostics while the fake-photon background interpretation remains blocked.

The separation of source profiles does not supply a validated overlap or matching prescription. The existing bbgg/ccgg/jjgg samples and the new bb gamma j component use matrix elements and shower emissions with different hard-photon multiplicities. A combined treatment would need defined prompt-photon origins, photon isolation and fragmentation conventions, perturbative orders, phase-space boundaries, and a compatible matching/merging or subtraction prescription. Possible continuum/resonant interference and the scope of the selected resonance diagrams must also be assessed before combining yields. No overlap correction, inclusive completion or double-counting removal is inferred from the process labels alone.

The detector remains `FAST_SIM_ATLAS_LIKE`, `NOT_OFFICIAL_ATLAS`: the original anti-kT R=0.4 card, photon/jet responses and native bit0 tag decisions are retained, with no pileup. Numerical integrity, ancestry checks, event correspondence and source-weight closure do not establish official ATLAS response or tagging calibration. Geometric photon-to-truth matches are diagnostics; they are not calibrated photon efficiencies or fake rates.

The exploratory Phase6C selection remains fixed. No cut is optimized on these 1,000-event pilot streams, and no luminosity is applied implicitly. Every raw contribution uses the full original source denominator, including selected-event rows and published 100-event subsets. Raw/source sums and model-BR-adjusted sums remain separate quantities. A zero-survivor category requires a nonzero upper limit or interval statement; it cannot be interpreted as zero physical background.

Interpretation gate O is `BLOCKED_UNCALIBRATED_PHOTON_FAKE_RATE`. Gate P is `UNKNOWN_AND_BLOCKED_FOR_COMBINED_YIELDS` because a validated background overlap/matching scheme has not been established. Gate Q is `BLOCKED_OFFICIAL_DETECTOR_AND_PRECISION_BR_CALIBRATION`. Detector simulation can pass its own physical-record gates while O/P/Q retain these limits. No official background normalization, NLO matched production, calibrated photon fake prediction, HH signal sensitivity or discovery significance follows from this pilot.
